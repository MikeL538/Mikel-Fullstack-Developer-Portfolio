Wióry Lecą!

A website for a custom furniture business. It presents services and a categorized project gallery, with Polish, German and English versions and quick contact options.

Context and goal

A custom furniture business needs a clear presentation of its services and completed work. Visitors should be able to move from a furniture category to relevant examples and contact options.

My scope of work

Implementing a responsive website, modular HTML templates, SCSS styles and TypeScript interactions. Extending the gallery with categories and pagination, supporting three languages and preparing the static page and asset build process.

Technical decisions

The site uses HTML and TypeScript without a frontend framework. Vite combines shared partials and translations into complete pages. Gallery content and pagination are present in HTML, so browsing categories does not require JavaScript.

What the app does

Services, cooperation information, contact details and project photos grouped into kitchens, wardrobes, bathrooms and built-ins. Visitors can switch between Polish, German and English and open larger image previews.

Technical challenge

Expanding the gallery required consistent images, categories, pagination and language versions. A shared image catalogue feeds generated pages and the sitemap, while compatibility handling takes old category links to their new addresses.

Result

Services and examples of completed work are available in one business website. Generated gallery pages support category browsing, and shared templates simplify maintaining the language versions.

Limitations and next steps

This is a service and contact website, without a furniture configurator or online checkout. Adding photos or changing content requires file updates and a rebuild; the project does not have a CMS.
