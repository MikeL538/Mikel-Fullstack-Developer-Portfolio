About me

I'm Michał Lipiak, a fullstack developer. I build business websites and web apps, from service presentations to forms, API integrations and data workflows.

I choose tools to fit the task. Moje Atelier uses React and TypeScript, Wióry Lecą! generates pages from HTML and TypeScript, and SLO reunion registration connects a simple form to Supabase.

I work on interfaces, application logic and preparation for publishing. Across projects, I develop validation, error handling and tests. I distinguish completed features from deployment dependencies such as client content and service configuration.
